Rules

Play proceeds clockwise: You → West → North → East. The winner of each trick leads the next trick. Follow suit. 2♣ leads the first trick. Hearts cannot be led until hearts are broken (unless you only have hearts). Each ♥ = 1 point and Q♠ = 13 points. On the first trick you cannot play a point card if you have a non-point alternative. Taking all 26 points is Shooting the Moon. ♦J has no special rule.
